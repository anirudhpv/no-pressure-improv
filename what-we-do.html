What we do

Six ways to play with us.

From two-hour jams to full-day workshops, here's everything No Pressure Improv runs, and what to expect from each.

Typically 2 hours

In-person improv jams

Unlike traditional improv jams, which focus on playing for an audience, our jams take inspiration from musical jams. A facilitator leads the jam around a theme, but the focus is on getting a bunch of improvisers together and having FUNNN. No pressure!

Typically 2 hours

Online jams

Like our in-person jams, but online and open to anyone, anywhere in the world. We focus on creating and keeping connections, whatever the distance. Formats we run regularly include:

  • The 5-Minute Club
  • Reading Room
  • Improv games
  • Online workshops
Typically 3 hours

Improv labs

A space to experiment with ideas and try exercises we haven't explored yet. It's a testing ground for turning an idea into a jam or a workshop. All we ask is that you come with an open mind.

Productions

Shows

Our long-term vision is to make doing improv as popular as going to the movies, and for people serious about the art form to be able to make a living from it. Putting on excellent productions only strengthens those goals.

The first step is hosting shows by enthusiastic ensembles, such as:

  • The Fishbowl
  • Mobius Trip
  • The 6-hour improv marathon
Typically half or full day

Workshops

We host a range of expert facilitators, both international and local, who teach improv for performance as well as applied improv. Workshops we've hosted include:

  • Long-form narrative improv
  • Character work
  • Treteau
  • Musical improv
  • Freestyle improvised rap
Typically 2 hours

Social events

We believe improv and creativity thrive best where people are friends and feel safe. We host social events to build exactly that. Past ones include:

  • Movie nights
  • Game nights
  • Watch-alongs
  • Karaoke nights
