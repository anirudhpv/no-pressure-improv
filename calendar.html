Good to know

Payment

Pay the minimum shown on the form by UPI to 8008833554-1@okbizaxis (No Pressure Improv, +91 80088 33554) or scan this code. Contributions over and above are dearly welcome. They help cover NPI's operational costs and remunerate our facilitators.

Tickets

  • No refunds once a ticket is booked before the session.
  • Tickets can be moved to another jam or another participant. Tell the organisers at least 24 hours prior.
  • If NPI cancels a session, you get a full refund.
  • NPI reserves the right of admission to all events.

Financial assistance

Cost shouldn't keep you out. Email team@nopressureimprov.com or DM an admin.

  • Sliding scale: pay a minimum of ₹200, up to the suggested fee.
  • Payment in parts: start with ₹100 and pay the rest in staggered weekly instalments.
  • Barter a skill: volunteer for production, facilitation, video editing, poster making, crochet merch and more.
